Social Deduction · Party Game

IMPOSTOR

Everyone knows the secret word. Except one. Talk, bluff, and vote them out before it's too late.

3–15 players · pass & play on one device
←
How to Play
1
Everyone passes the phone around. Each civilian sees the same secret word and its category.
2
The imposter(s) see no word — only "YOU ARE THE IMPOSTOR", plus an optional category hint.
3
Take turns saying one word or clue related to the secret word. The imposter must bluff convincingly.
4
Discuss and debate using the timer, then everyone votes on who they think the imposter is.
5
If the group votes out an imposter, civilians win. If they vote wrong, the imposter escapes.
